# CV Bullet

> Analysed 3,421,083 orders from 206,209 repeat customers to identify early behaviours associated with becoming a heavy user (>= 20 orders); found early return pace strongly differentiated heavy users, while basket-composition effects were negligible to small (lead OR 1.14 [95% CI 1.13–1.16] for produce share (1st order); all basket Cohen's d < 0.20); proposed 3 A/B test designs targeting second-order timing with defined success and guardrail metrics.
